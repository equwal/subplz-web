Terms, privacy and refunds

Short, and meant to be read. Contact: see below.

What this is

SubRead lines an audiobook up with its ebook and gives you timed subtitles, videos and a read-along book. All of the code is public, and you may run it yourself.

  • In your browser (free): the work is done on your machine. Your audio and your book are not uploaded. The server gets the names and sizes of the files and the finished subtitles, so that this page can list your conversions.
  • On our server (credits): you upload the audio and the book, and the server does the work. One credit converts one book. New visitors get some free credits to try it.

Your files

  • Use only files that you have the right to use. You keep all rights in them. We claim none.
  • Files uploaded for a server conversion are deleted when the job succeeds. If a job fails, is cancelled, or is never started, they are kept for 24 hours so that you can try again, and then deleted.
  • Results (subtitles, videos) are kept for 7 days and then deleted. Download what you want to keep.
  • Nobody looks at your files, they are not shared, and they are not used to train anything. There is no public library of uploads.

Payments and refunds

  • Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see your card.
  • Credits do not expire.
  • Free credits have no cash value. We do not refund them.
  • A job that fails or that you cancel gives its credit back by itself.
  • If a result is not usable (for example the subtitles do not match the book), write to us within 14 days and we return the credit or the money, as you choose. Try the free conversion in your browser first: it uses the same method, and it shows whether your book and your audio match.
  • Unused credits are refunded on request within 14 days of the purchase.

Accounts and privacy

  • Without an account, a cookie keeps your conversions together. It holds a random identifier and nothing else. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • An account is an email address. You get one when you pay (Stripe collects the address) or when you ask for a sign-in link. There are no passwords. We use the address for sign-in links and for answers to your messages, and for nothing else.
  • We store: your email address (if you gave one), your credits and purchases, and the list of your conversions (file names, sizes, language, times). Write to us to have all of it deleted.
  • The server keeps ordinary web logs (IP address, time, page) for a short time, for security.

Copyright complaints

We do not publish or share what users upload, and uploads are deleted as said above. If you believe that material on this service infringes your copyright, write to the contact address with: the work, where it is on this service, your contact details, a statement that you act in good faith for the owner of the right, and your signature. We remove what the notice properly names, and we close the accounts of repeat infringers.

No warranty

The subtitles are made by a machine that matches a rough transcript to your book. They are usually good and never perfect. The service is given as it is, without warranty. Our liability is limited to what you paid in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing here limits rights that the law of your country gives you as a consumer.
