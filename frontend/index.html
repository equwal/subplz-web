SubRead

Line an audiobook up with its ebook, sentence by sentence.

Drop in an audiobook and the ebook it was read from. You get back subtitles timed to the narration, with the wording taken from your own book — not from a machine's guess at what it heard. The work happens in this tab, on your computer.

  • HoshiReader read-along — the .srt timing file, to match against your epub. free
  • Read-along book — your epub with the narration inside it (EPUB 3 Media Overlays). Thorium, Storyteller and other EPUB 3 readers highlight each line as it is read. free
  • Local video — an .mkv with the subtitles built in, for MPV or VLC. free
  • YouTube video — a clean .mp4, plus the .srt to upload as its captions. free

Free in your browser, without limit.
